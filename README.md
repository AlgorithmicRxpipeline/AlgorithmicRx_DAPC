# AlgorithmicRx_DAPC
**DAPC** is an in silico target-discovery pipeline developed by AlgorithmicRx, 
focused initially on the β-dystroglycan interface in Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD).

## Overview
DAPC combines multiple open-source protein structure prediction models — including 
AlphaFold 3, ESMFold2, OpenFold3, and Boltz-1/Boltz-2 — to generate and cross-validate 
structural hypotheses at the β-dystroglycan interface. Claude is used throughout the 
pipeline to run bioinformatics workflows, analyze multi-omics data, and mine clinical 
and scientific literature on DMD and related muscular dystrophies to infer shared 
downstream pathology and network biology across diseases.

## Status
DAPC is currently at MVP stage. Structural and mechanistic hypotheses generated by 
the pipeline have not yet undergone experimental (wet-lab) validation.

## Approach
- Multi-model protein structure prediction (model-agnostic, not reliant on a single tool)
- Literature and omics-informed network biology inference across muscular dystrophies
- Claude-assisted analysis and hypothesis generation, with human expert review

## About AlgorithmicRx
AlgorithmicRx is an AI-driven drug discovery company focused on accelerating 
therapeutic development for rare diseases, beginning with Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy.
